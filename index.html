Settings

Practice

Everyday phrases are mixed into the lesson, about every other item. The chunks follow grammatikk.com’s preposition list, Birger’s idiom list, Norsk for deg on an, NTNU LearnNoW extras, and dictionary entries for få, jo, vel, da and nok. The sentences here are ours. A phrase is not a grammar score. Jo is not “yes”, vel is not “well”, and nok in these turns is not “enough”.
